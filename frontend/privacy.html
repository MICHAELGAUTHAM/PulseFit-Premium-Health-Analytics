Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At PulseFit, we value your privacy above all. This Privacy Policy details how we handle your biometrics, user accounts, and personal profile data when you interact with our local health dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

We collect credentials (email and hashed passwords) to protect account access. In addition, we collect biometric parameters you input (height, weight, age, steps walked, calories burned, sleep hours, water consumed, and resting heart rates) to calculate your Health Index and generate analytics.

2. How We Use Data

We use your data strictly to display history trends, score goals, and trigger achievements locally. We do NOT lease, distribute, or share your fitness logs or personal parameters with external marketing agencies or third parties.

3. Data Deletion and Revocation

You have complete control over your files. You can export your health logs in JSON format or delete your account at any time. Clicking "Delete Account" in settings triggers an immediate cascade that purges all user rows, health logs, and badge progress from our local databases.

4. Security Practices

Passwords are encrypted immediately upon registration using secure bcrypt hashes. Sessions are protected via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) which expire regularly. All database connections are protected against SQL injections.
