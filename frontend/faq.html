Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding tracker biometrics, account settings, and features.

Your Daily Health Index score (percentage) is computed dynamically based on your progress towards four core targets: your hydration target (ml), your active step count goal, your calorie output threshold, and your logged sleep duration. Reaching all four targets yields a 100% Index score.

Streaks and achievements are evaluated by our backend engine on every biometric update. For example, logging daily data for 7 consecutive days unlocks the "7-Day Streak" badge, while logging water intake above 2.5L triggers the "Hydration Master" badge. Unlocks issue system notifications immediately.

You can adjust display settings in the Settings panel. Checking "Metric Units" formats your height in centimeters (cm) and weight in kilograms (kg). Unchecking switches to Imperial formatting (inches and pounds). Calculations automatically translate between representations.

Security is our priority. PulseFit hashes all credentials using industry-grade bcrypt algorithms and issues stateless, secure JWT (JSON Web Tokens) to authenticate API requests. Route endpoints require tokens to verify access permissions, blocking SQL injection attempts.
